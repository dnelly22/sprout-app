Sprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · Sprout by Alenlor

The short version: Sprout does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Everything your family does in the app stays on your device. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no third‑party tracking.

Who this app is for

Sprout is a parenting and kid‑confidence companion. A parent or guardian is the account holder; children use a PIN‑gated “Kid Zone.” It is designed to be used by families together.

Information we collect

None that leaves your device. Sprout stores your progress (child profiles you create, stars, lesson and game progress, settings) only in your device’s local storage. This information is never sent to us or to any third party. We have no servers that receive your data.

What children see and do

The Kid Zone is fully scripted. Children never interact with an AI chatbot and cannot enter free text that is sent anywhere. Every word a child sees is pre‑written content shipped inside the app.

No third‑party services, ads, or analytics

Sprout contains no third‑party advertising, no behavioral tracking, and no analytics SDKs. We do not use cookies for tracking.

Purchases

The current version has no in‑app purchases or payments.

Data deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. There is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Sprout is designed to comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal information for us to handle.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data (for example, by adding optional cloud sync or accounts), we will update this policy and clearly describe the change before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@alenlor.com.
