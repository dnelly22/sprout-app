Sprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026 · Sprout is operated by Alenlor, LLC

The short version: The Sprout app keeps your family’s data on your device and shows no ads and no third‑party tracking or analytics. We only ever share the minimum needed to run the things you ask for — process a purchase, or answer an “Ask Sprout” question. Children never provide personal information.

Who Sprout is for

Sprout is a parenting and kid‑confidence app. A parent or guardian is the account holder and sets up the experience; children use a PIN‑gated “Kid Zone.” Sprout is intended for use by families together and is not directed at children as the purchasing or account‑holding user.

Information we collect

Stored on your device. When you set up Sprout you may enter your name and email and your child’s first name and age. This, along with your progress (stars, lessons, game activity, settings), is kept in your device’s local storage. In the app it is not transmitted to us or used for advertising.

Purchases. If you subscribe in the iOS app, the purchase is handled by Apple’s In‑App Purchase. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify and manage your subscription status; RevenueCat receives purchase information and an app‑generated identifier solely to keep your subscription active across your devices. This is used for app functionality only — never for advertising, and it is not used to track you.

“Ask Sprout.” If a parent uses the optional “Ask Sprout” helper, the message you type is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to return a lesson recommendation. Please don’t include sensitive personal details; the feature is for guidance, not diagnosis. Children do not have access to it.

What children see and do

The Kid Zone is fully scripted. Children never talk to an AI, cannot enter free text that is sent anywhere, and are shown only pre‑written content shipped inside the app. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and there is no advertising or third‑party tracking anywhere in the app.

No third‑party advertising or tracking in the app

The Sprout iOS app contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, and no analytics SDKs. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency because we do not track you.

Our website

Separately from the app, our sign‑up website (alenlor.com) uses standard advertising and measurement tools (including Meta’s advertising tools) and, if you request your plan, stores the name and email you submit so we can follow up. Payments taken on the website are processed by Stripe. These website practices do not apply inside the app.

Who we share data with

  • Apple — processes in‑app purchases.
  • RevenueCat — manages your subscription status.
  • Anthropic — processes “Ask Sprout” messages you send.
  • Stripe and Meta — website only (payments and advertising), as above.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data deletion

Because your app data lives on your device, you can delete all of it any time from Settings → Reset app data, or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of anything held by our service providers (such as a website sign‑up or a subscription record), email support@alenlor.com and we will handle it promptly.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Sprout is designed to comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Any information about a child (such as a first name or age) is entered by a parent, stays on the device, and is never used for advertising or shared with advertisers. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sprout handles data (for example, by adding accounts or cloud sync), we will update this policy and describe the change before it takes effect.
