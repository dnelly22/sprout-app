## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 9
- **Title:** Followers
- **Communication skill:** Healthy following vs. going along blindly
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Not everyone has to be the leader — being a good team member and supporting good ideas is valuable too! But there's a difference between *healthy* following (backing a good plan, being a great teammate) and following *blindly* (going along with whatever the group does, even when it's wrong). Reading this in yourself and others means knowing when to follow a good lead — and when to stop and think for yourself.
