## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 4
- **Title:** Active Listening
- **Communication skill:** Truly listening so people feel heard
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Listening is a superpower most people never actually learn. *Real* listening means putting down what you're doing, looking at the person, not planning your reply while they talk, and showing you heard them. People can feel the difference instantly — and they love whoever truly listens to them. It's one of the kindest, most powerful things you can do, and it costs nothing but your attention.

## 🌟 Final Reflection  `id: reflection-h`

> "Grace was in real pain — and you helped most by *not* trying to fix it. 🌱 The hardest listening is when someone's hurting and every instinct says solve it, cheer them up, give advice.
