## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 10
- **Title:** Speaking in Class
- **Communication skill:** Speaking up in a group/classroom
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Speaking up in class is scary — *what if I'm wrong? what if people laugh?* But your voice and your ideas matter, and the more you use them, the easier it gets. Here's the secret: you don't have to be right or brilliant, just willing to share. Teachers love participation, wrong answers help the whole class learn, and the kids who speak up build a confidence that lasts way beyond the classroom.
