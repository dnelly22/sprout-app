## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 3
- **Title:** Quiet People
- **Communication skill:** Understanding that quiet is a personality, not a problem
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Some people are just *quiet* — they don't need to talk a lot, and that's totally fine. Quiet isn't the same as shy (nervous), sad, or rude, and it doesn't mean something's wrong. Quiet people are often great listeners, thoughtful, and observant — they notice things loud people miss. Reading quiet people right means you won't try to "fix" them or push them to be someone they're not — and you'll appreciate what they bring.
