## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 2
- **Title:** Starting Small Talk
- **Communication skill:** Making easy, friendly small talk
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Small talk is the warm-up to every friendship — the easy, no-pressure chatter about the line, the weather, the game, whatever's around you. It shows you're friendly and opens the door to bigger conversations. The best part: it does *not* have to be deep, clever, or impressive. The whole secret is to comment on something you both can see, keep it light, and ask an easy question. "Boring but real" beats "clever but stiff" every single time.
