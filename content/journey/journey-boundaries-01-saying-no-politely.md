## Boundaries · Scenario 1 — "Saying No Politely"

> **New category!** Same structure/schema as the flagship (journey-friends-01): three modes, 5 beats + repair + reflection, four choices (A ★★★★★/+25 · B ★★★★/+15 · C ★★/+8 · D ★/+3→repair), named micro-skills, XP, traits.
>
> **About this category:** A boundary is a line that protects you — your time, your stuff, your space, your body, your "no." This category is the practical toolkit: the actual words and moves for *setting* your boundaries, *holding* them when people push, and *respecting* other people's boundaries too. First up — the most important word of all: **no.**

## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 1
- **Title:** Saying No Politely
- **Communication skill:** A clear, kind "no"
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  "No" is one of the most important words you'll ever learn to say. You're allowed to say no — to things you don't want to do, don't have time for, or don't feel right about. And you can do it *politely:* clear and kind at the same time. You don't have to be rude, and you don't have to over-explain or apologize a hundred times. A firm "no, thanks" is a complete answer — and saying it doesn't make you mean or a bad friend.
