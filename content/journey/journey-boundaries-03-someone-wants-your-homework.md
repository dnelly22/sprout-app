## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 3
- **Title:** Someone Wants Your Homework
- **Communication skill:** Declining to let someone copy — while still being a good friend
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Sometimes a kid will ask to copy your homework. It can feel hard to say no — you don't want to seem mean or unhelpful. But letting someone copy isn't fair to you, it doesn't actually help them learn, and it can get you BOTH in trouble. The good news: you can say no AND still be a good friend — by offering *real* help (explaining it) instead of answers.
