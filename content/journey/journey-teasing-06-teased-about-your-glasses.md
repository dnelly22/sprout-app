## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 6
- **Title:** Teased About Your Glasses
- **Communication skill:** Reframing a "flaw" into pride
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  People sometimes tease about glasses, braces, or other things that aren't even bad — they're just part of you, or honestly kind of cool. The move here: own it, and even flip it into a positive. "Yeah, I wear glasses — I think they look great, actually." When you genuinely *like* the thing they're teasing, there's nothing left to tease. You get to decide how you feel about what makes you, *you.*
