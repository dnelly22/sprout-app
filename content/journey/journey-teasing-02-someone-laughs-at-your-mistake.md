## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 2
- **Title:** Someone Laughs at Your Mistake
- **Communication skill:** Owning mistakes with confidence
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Everyone makes mistakes — tripping, flubbing a word, getting an answer wrong. When people laugh, you can crumble with embarrassment, OR you can own it and even laugh along, which takes all the sting right out. Mistakes don't make you less — they make you human. And here's the twist: the kid who can laugh at their own slip-up looks *more* confident than the kid who acts like they never mess up.
