## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 18
- **Title:** Encouraging a Friend
- **Communication skill:** Positive support
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  One of the best things about having a friend is having someone in your corner — someone who believes in you out loud. Encouraging a friend (cheering them on, lifting them up when they're down, believing in them when they can't) is a gift you can give over and over, and it costs nothing. Sometimes your belief in someone is the exact push they need to do something brave. Be the person who builds others up.
