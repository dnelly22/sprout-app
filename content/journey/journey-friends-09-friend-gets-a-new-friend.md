## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 9
- **Title:** Friend Gets a New Friend
- **Communication skill:** Handling jealousy
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When your friend makes a *new* friend, your stomach can do a weird, twisty thing — that's jealousy, and it's totally normal. But here's the truth: friendship isn't pizza. There isn't a limited number of slices. Your friend liking someone new doesn't mean they like you less. Learning to handle that twisty feeling — instead of letting it make you act cold or possessive — is what keeps your friendships *and* lets your circle grow.
