## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 4
- **Title:** Competitive People
- **Communication skill:** Reading and navigating competitiveness
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Some people are really competitive — they love to win and make lots of things a contest. That energy can be fun and motivating! But sometimes competitiveness goes too far — into sore losing, gloating, cheating, or ruining the fun. Reading a competitive person means knowing the difference between fun-competitive and too-far-competitive: enjoy the good kind, and don't get sucked into someone's need to win at all costs.
