## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 11
- **Title:** Feeling Angry
- **Communication skill:** Handling anger without hurting anyone
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Anger is a normal, healthy feeling — it's okay to BE angry, and anger often means something matters to you or feels unfair. The important thing isn't whether you feel angry, it's what you DO with it. You can feel anger fully without hurting people, things, or yourself. The skill: notice the anger, cool down before you act (pause, breathe, step away), and then express it with words instead of fists or yelling.
