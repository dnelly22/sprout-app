## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 4
- **Title:** Someone Wants to Cheat
- **Communication skill:** Refusing to be part of cheating
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Cheating — on a test, a quiz, anything — might seem like a quick fix, but it's a line worth not crossing. Someone might want to copy off you during a test, ask you to slip them answers, or pressure you to cheat together. Saying no protects your integrity (and keeps you out of serious trouble). You can refuse to be part of cheating — even when it's a friend, even under pressure — and walk away from a scheme that isn't yours.
