## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 3
- **Title:** Someone Calls You a Name
- **Communication skill:** Refusing a label someone tries to stick on you
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Name-calling is one of the oldest tricks — and it only works if *you* believe the name. A mean nickname or insult only sticks if you accept it as true. When you stay calm and refuse the label ("that's not who I am"), the name loses all its power. Here's the bottom line: *you* decide who you are. Not the person calling you names.
