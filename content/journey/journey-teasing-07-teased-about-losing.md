## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 7
- **Title:** Teased About Losing
- **Communication skill:** Staying secure when someone rubs in a loss
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Sometimes when you lose, the other person doesn't just win — they rub it in, gloat, or tease you about it. That can sting and make you want to snap or feel like "a loser." But losing at a game doesn't make you one, and a gloating winner is *hoping* you'll get upset. Stay secure — "good game, you won this one" — and don't give them the rise they want. How you handle losing says way more about you than the score does.
