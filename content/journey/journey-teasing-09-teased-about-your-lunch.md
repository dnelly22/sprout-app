## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 9
- **Title:** Teased About Your Lunch
- **Communication skill:** Pride in your culture and what makes you different
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Sometimes kids tease about food that's different from theirs — calling it "weird" or "smelly." Usually that's food from your culture or family — food that's actually delicious and special to you. Here's the truth: different isn't weird, it's *interesting.* Your culture and your family's food are something to be PROUD of, not ashamed of. Never let someone make you hide a part of who you are.
