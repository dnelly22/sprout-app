## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 8
- **Title:** Joining a Conversation
- **Communication skill:** Entering an ongoing conversation smoothly
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Walking up to people who are already talking can feel intimidating — you don't want to interrupt or seem like you're butting in. But there's a smooth way: get close, listen for a beat to catch the topic, then add something relevant or ask a question. Done right, joining is easy and welcome — most people are happy to include you. The whole trick is catching the thread *before* you jump in.
