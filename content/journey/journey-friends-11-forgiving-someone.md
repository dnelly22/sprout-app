## Friends · Scenario 11 — "Forgiving Someone"

> Same structure as the flagship (Friends #1). All three modes fully built. This is the flip side of #10 — now *you're* the one who was hurt.

## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 11
- **Title:** Forgiving Someone
- **Communication skill:** Repairing friendships
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When someone hurts you and says a real sorry, *forgiving* them is a gift — but here's the secret: it's mostly a gift to *yourself.* Holding a grudge is like carrying a heavy backpack of rocks; the only person it weighs down is you. Forgiving doesn't mean what they did was okay, and it doesn't mean you can't have a boundary. It just means choosing to set the rocks down and move forward.
