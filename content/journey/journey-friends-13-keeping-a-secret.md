## Friends · Scenario 13 — "Keeping a Secret"

> Same structure as the flagship (Friends #1). All three modes fully built.
> ⚠️ Hard mode teaches the safe-secret / unsafe-secret distinction — an important safety lesson, handled gently.

## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 13
- **Title:** Keeping a Secret
- **Communication skill:** Building trust
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When a friend trusts you with a secret, keeping it is how you prove you're a real friend. Being *trustworthy* — being someone whose word is good — is one of the most valuable things about a person. People can feel safe with you. **One big exception, though:** some "secrets" aren't safe to keep. If a secret is about someone getting *hurt* or being in *danger,* the caring thing is to tell a trusted grown-up. Learning the difference between a safe secret and an unsafe one is one of the most important things you'll ever learn.
