## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 5
- **Title:** Bossy People
- **Communication skill:** Standing up to bossiness without being aggressive
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Some people get bossy — they tell everyone what to do, take over, and act like their way is the only way. Here's the key: you don't have to follow orders just because someone's pushy! Reading a bossy person means knowing you can stand up for yourself calmly, share your own ideas, and not get steamrolled — while still telling the difference between someone being *controlling* and someone just trying to *lead and help.*
