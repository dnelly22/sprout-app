## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 3
- **Title:** Asking Great Questions
- **Communication skill:** Asking open, curious questions
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Here's a surprise: the best conversationalists aren't the ones who talk the most — they're the ones who *ask the best questions.* A good question turns a boring "fine" into a real story, makes people light up, and shows you actually care. The trick is simple: ask "what" and "how" instead of yes-or-no questions, get specific, and follow up on whatever they say. Be *interested,* and you'll never run out of things to talk about.
