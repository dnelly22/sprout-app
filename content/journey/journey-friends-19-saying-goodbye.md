## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 19
- **Title:** Saying Goodbye
- **Communication skill:** Handling change & loss in friendships
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Friendships change. Someone moves away, switches schools, or just slowly drifts in a different direction. Saying goodbye is sad — but how you handle it matters. You can honor what the friendship meant, stay in touch when you can, and let go gracefully when you need to. Change is part of life, and it never erases what a friendship was. Learning to say goodbye well means you can love people fully *and* let them go when it's time.
