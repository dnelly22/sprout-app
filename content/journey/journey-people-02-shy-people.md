## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 2
- **Title:** Shy People
- **Communication skill:** Reading shyness and responding gently
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Shy people aren't unfriendly or stuck-up — they just feel nervous around new people, especially in groups. If you understand that shyness isn't about *you,* you can help a shy person feel comfortable: be patient, go gentle, give them time, and include them without pressure. Reading shyness right means you won't miss out on great friends who just take a little while to open up.
