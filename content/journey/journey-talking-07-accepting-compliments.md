## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 7
- **Title:** Accepting Compliments
- **Communication skill:** Receiving a compliment graciously
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When someone compliments you, the kind, confident thing is simply to say "thank you." But lots of people deflect ("oh, it was nothing"), argue ("no, I'm terrible at this"), or get awkward — which accidentally *rejects* the gift someone just gave you. Accepting a compliment graciously honors the person who gave it AND lets you actually feel good. Here's permission you might need: you are allowed to feel proud of yourself.
