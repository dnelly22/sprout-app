## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 14
- **Title:** Someone Breaks Your Trust
- **Communication skill:** Rebuilding trust (and knowing when to step back)
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When someone breaks your trust, it's confusing — should you forgive and trust them again, or protect yourself? Here's the secret most people never learn: **trust and forgiveness are two different things.** You can forgive someone (let go of the anger) AND still decide not to count on them the same way. A one-time mistake with a real sorry? Give another chance. The same letdown over and over, even after promises? It's okay to step back. Trust is earned back through *actions,* not words.
