## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 14
- **Title:** Feeling Sad
- **Communication skill:** Letting yourself feel sad, and reaching out
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Sadness is a normal, important feeling — everyone feels it, and it's nothing to be ashamed of or hide. When you're sad — a bad day, a disappointment, a loss, or just feeling down — the healthy thing is to let yourself feel it (not bottle it up or pretend you're fine), be gentle with yourself, do comforting things, and reach out to someone instead of going through it alone.
