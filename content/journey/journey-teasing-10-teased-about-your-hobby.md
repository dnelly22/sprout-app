## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 10
- **Title:** Teased About Your Hobby
- **Communication skill:** Owning what you love (unapologetic enthusiasm)
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  People sometimes tease the things you're into — calling a hobby "weird," "babyish," "nerdy," or "lame." But here's the thing: liking what you like is one of the *best* parts of being you. The world is full of cool stuff, and loving something — anything — with your whole heart is awesome, not embarrassing. Never let someone shame you out of a joy. Let people like things.
