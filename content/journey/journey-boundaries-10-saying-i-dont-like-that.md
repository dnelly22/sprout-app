## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 10
- **Title:** Saying "I Don't Like That"
- **Communication skill:** Naming your discomfort clearly
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When someone does something that bothers you — a "joke" at your expense, a poke, a habit, anything that crosses a line — you don't have to just sit with it. One of the simplest, most powerful things you can say is: "I don't like that." Clear, honest, no big explanation needed. Naming your discomfort out loud is how people learn where your lines are.
