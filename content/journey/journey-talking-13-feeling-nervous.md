## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 13
- **Title:** Feeling Nervous
- **Communication skill:** Managing nerves and worry
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Feeling nervous before something big — a test, a performance, a new situation, meeting new people — is completely normal. Nervousness actually means you CARE about something. The trick isn't to make the nerves disappear (almost impossible), it's to *manage* them: name the feeling, take some calming breaths, remind yourself you're prepared, and do the brave thing anyway. Courage isn't feeling no fear — it's doing it even while you're nervous.
