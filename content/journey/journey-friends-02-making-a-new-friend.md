## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 2
- **Title:** Making a New Friend
- **Communication skill:** Introducing yourself and asking follow-up questions
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  A friend is just a stranger you said hi to. The two tiny skills that turn "some kid" into "my friend" are *telling them your name* and *asking them questions.* Questions are like little gifts — they tell the other person "I'm interested in you." Once you can do those two things, you can make a friend anywhere: a new school, a park, a birthday party, a summer camp.

# EASY MODE — "The New Kid"

**The cast:** **Ben** (just moved here, nervous, secretly loves drawing).
**Setting:** recess. There's a new kid standing by the wall, watching everyone like he's trying to learn the rules of a new planet. You remember that feeling.
