## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 3
- **Title:** Feeling Left Out
- **Communication skill:** Speaking up respectfully
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Feeling left out is one of the worst feelings there is — and it makes your brain want to do two things: hide and sulk, or get mad and lash out. Neither one gets you back in. The real skill is *speaking up calmly:* checking what's actually going on, saying how you feel without blaming, and asking to be included. Most of the time, you weren't left out on purpose at all — and even when you were, how you handle it decides whether you keep your friends and your pride.
