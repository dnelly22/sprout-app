## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 6
- **Title:** Giving Compliments
- **Communication skill:** Giving genuine, specific compliments
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  A good compliment can make someone's whole day. But generic ones ("nice job") kind of bounce off — the secret is being *specific* (say exactly what you noticed) and *genuine.* The best compliments are about effort, character, or choices — not just looks — and you give them to be kind, not to get one back. Lifting people up costs you nothing and makes you someone everyone loves to be around.
