## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 15
- **Title:** Working Together
- **Communication skill:** Teamwork
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  So much of life happens *with* other people — group projects, sports teams, games, clubs. Working together well means making a plan, sharing the work, building on each other's ideas, handling it when someone's not pulling their weight, and winning *and* losing as a team. A great teammate makes everyone around them better. That's a skill people will want from you your whole life.
