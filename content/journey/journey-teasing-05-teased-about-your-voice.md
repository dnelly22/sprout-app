## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 5
- **Title:** Teased About Your Voice
- **Communication skill:** Not letting teasing silence you
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Your voice is yours — how it sounds, whether it's high or low, if it cracks, your accent, all of it. People can tease about it, but here's the sneaky thing voice-teasing especially wants to do: make you afraid to *talk.* Don't let it. Your voice is how you share your ideas, your jokes, your whole self with the world. Stay confident and keep using it — the way you sound is part of what makes you *you.*
