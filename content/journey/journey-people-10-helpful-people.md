## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 10
- **Title:** Helpful People
- **Communication skill:** Appreciating help, accepting it graciously, and helping in a healthy way
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Helpful people are some of the kindest to have around — they notice when you need something and step in. Reading helpfulness means appreciating helpful people and accepting help graciously (without being too proud to take it). And if YOU'RE the helpful one — that's wonderful, but healthy helping has limits: help because you care (not to be liked), respect when someone doesn't want help, and remember to take care of yourself too.
