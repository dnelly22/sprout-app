## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 6
- **Title:** Protecting Your Belongings
- **Communication skill:** Standing up to keep your things safe
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Your belongings are yours, and you have the right to keep them safe. Sometimes someone takes your stuff and won't give it back, keeps "forgetting" to return things, or messes with your things. It's okay to stand up and get your stuff back — calmly and clearly. And when "borrowing" is really *taking* — when someone takes something with no intention of returning it — that's different, and it's okay to get an adult's help.
