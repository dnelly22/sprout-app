## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 12
- **Title:** Talking on the Phone
- **Communication skill:** Phone calls — answering, calling, messages
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Talking on the phone is a different skill than talking in person — the other person can't see you, so your *voice* does all the work. Knowing how to answer politely, make a call, take a clear message, and not freeze up is genuinely useful: calling a friend, a grandparent, asking for something, or handling a call for your family. It feels awkward at first, but it's just practice — and a few simple habits make it easy.

# EASY MODE — "Making the Call"

**The cast:** **Wade** (your friend) and **his mom**.
**Setting:** you want to call Wade to see if he can hang out.
