## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 2
- **Title:** Borrowing Without Asking
- **Communication skill:** Setting a boundary around your belongings
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Your things are yours — and it's totally okay to expect people to ASK before using or taking them. When someone borrows without asking, you're allowed to speak up (calmly and clearly). And it works both ways: you should ask before using other people's stuff, too. Respecting "ask first" keeps everyone's stuff — and feelings — safe.
