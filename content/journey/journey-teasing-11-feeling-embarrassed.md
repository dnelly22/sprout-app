## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 11
- **Title:** Feeling Embarrassed
- **Communication skill:** Handling embarrassment with self-compassion
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Everyone feels embarrassed sometimes — you trip, you say the wrong thing, something awkward happens in front of people. That hot, want-to-disappear feeling is totally normal. The good news: embarrassment feels HUGE in the moment but fades fast, and how you handle it matters way more than the awkward thing itself. You can let it pass, even laugh at yourself, and move on — and most people forget about it way sooner than you think.

# HARD MODE — "When It Won't Let Go"
