## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 10
- **Title:** Apologizing
- **Communication skill:** Taking responsibility
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Everybody messes up — that's not the question. The question is what you do *after.* A real apology has four parts: own it (no excuses), say sorry like you mean it, make it right, and do better next time. A *fake* apology ("sorry you got upset," "sorry, but…") actually makes things worse. Learning to give a real one is one of the most powerful friendship skills there is — it can fix almost anything.
