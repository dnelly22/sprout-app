## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 9
- **Title:** Keeping a Conversation Going
- **Communication skill:** Sustaining a conversation so it doesn't die
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Lots of people can *start* a conversation but panic the second it stalls. Keeping one going isn't about being interesting — it's about being *interested.* You've got three easy moves: ask a follow-up, share a little of your own and hand it back, and when a topic runs out, pivot to a new one. And here's the relief: silences are totally normal, not emergencies. One good question can restart almost anything.
