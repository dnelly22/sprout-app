## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 6
- **Title:** Taking Turns
- **Communication skill:** Patience and fairness
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Taking turns is the invisible rule that makes games fun for *everyone* instead of just one kid. It has two parts: the patience to wait without whining, and the fairness to make sure others get a turn too — even when you'd rather keep going. The kids who are good at turns are the ones everyone wants to play with, because games near them are always fair.
