## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 7
- **Title:** Funny People
- **Communication skill:** Reading humor — funny WITH vs. funny AT
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Funny people are some of the best to be around — they make everyone laugh and lighten the mood. But there are different kinds of humor: the great kind makes people laugh WITH each other (and even with themselves), and the not-great kind gets laughs AT someone's expense. Reading the difference helps you enjoy genuine funny people, *not* laugh along when "jokes" are really mean, and — if you're the funny one — use your humor to lift people up, not put them down.
