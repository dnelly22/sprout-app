## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 22
- **Title:** Feeling Jealous
- **Communication skill:** Handling jealousy without letting it turn mean
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Jealousy — that twangy feeling when someone has something you want, gets something you wanted, or seems "better" at something — is totally normal. Everyone feels it. It's not a "bad" feeling, but it can push you toward bad stuff (resentment, meanness, putting others down) if you let it run. The healthy move: notice it, don't act on it badly, use it as a clue about what YOU want, and practice being genuinely happy for others. Comparison is a trap — your path is your own.
