## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 12
- **Title:** Helping a Lonely Child
- **Communication skill:** Including others
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Look around any playground and there's almost always a kid sitting alone — watching, wishing someone would notice them. Being the person who *notices* and reaches out is one of the kindest things you can ever do, and it costs you nothing. One invitation, one "want to sit with us?", can change someone's entire day. The best part: you grow your own circle of friends every time you do it.

# HARD MODE — "When Someone's Hurting"

**Setting:** recess. Jordan used to hang out with everyone. Lately he sits alone, staring at nothing. Today he looks really down.
