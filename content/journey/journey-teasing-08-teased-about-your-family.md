## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 8
- **Title:** Teased About Your Family
- **Communication skill:** Defending your family calmly, without taking the bait
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Family teasing hits different — because you love your family and feel protective of them. Someone teasing your mom, your sibling, where you live, or your family situation can make you want to *fight.* It's okay to firmly defend your family ("don't talk about my family like that"), but don't let them provoke you into a brawl — that's often exactly what they want. And when teasing about your family gets cruel about something real and hard, tell a trusted adult.
