## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 5
- **Title:** Respecting Personal Space
- **Communication skill:** Asking for your space — and respecting others'
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Everyone has a personal "bubble" — the space around their body that's theirs. It's okay to want your space, and to ask for it when someone's too close, crowding you, or touching/hugging you when you don't want it. It's just as important to RESPECT other people's space — to read when someone wants room, and to take a "no" to a hug or touch seriously. Your body and your space are yours; theirs are theirs.
