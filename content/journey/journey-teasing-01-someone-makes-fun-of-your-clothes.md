## Teasing · Scenario 1 — "Someone Makes Fun of Your Clothes"

> **New category!** Same structure/schema as the flagship (journey-friends-01): three modes, 5 beats + repair + reflection each, four choices (A ★★★★★/+25 · B ★★★★/+15 · C ★★/+8 · D ★/+3→repair), named micro-skills, XP, traits. Poor choices route to repair, never fail.
>
> *Note on this category: the strong responses are always calm, confident, and secure — never "fire back a meaner insult." Teasing skills = don't give it power, stay secure, set a boundary, walk away, and get a trusted adult when it turns cruel.*

## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 1
- **Title:** Someone Makes Fun of Your Clothes
- **Communication skill:** Not giving teasing your reaction
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Here's the secret about teasing: it runs on *your reaction.* A teaser wants to see you get upset, embarrassed, or mad — that's the prize. When you stay calm and unbothered (a shrug, a "so what," just not caring), the teasing runs out of fuel and gets boring fast. You can't always control what people say. But you completely control how much power you hand them.
