> **New category!** Same structure and schema as the Friends flagship (journey-friends-01): three modes (Easy/Medium/Hard), 5 beats + 1 repair + reflection each, four choices (A=★★★★★/+25 · B=★★★★/+15 · C=★★/+8 · D=★/+3→repair), named micro-skills, XP, and traits. ## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 1
- **Title:** Introducing Yourself
- **Communication skill:** Making a confident first introduction
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Introducing yourself is the doorway to every new friend, team, and group. It feels scary — but a simple, confident "Hi, I'm ___" opens almost any door. Here's the secret: you don't have to be clever, funny, or cool. You just have to be friendly and brave enough to go first. The kid who says hi first looks confident *and* kind — and usually ends up with the friends.
