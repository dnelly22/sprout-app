## People · Scenario 1 — "Friendly People"

> **New category!** Same structure/schema as the flagship (journey-friends-01): three modes, 5 beats + repair + reflection, four choices (A ★★★★★/+25 · B ★★★★/+15 · C ★★/+8 · D ★/+3→repair), named micro-skills, XP, traits.
>
> **About this category:** People aren't all the same — they think, feel, and act in different ways. Learning to *read* someone before you react is a superpower: it helps you respond wisely instead of guessing wrong. Each scenario is about understanding a kind of person and responding well. First up: friendly people.

## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 1
- **Title:** Friendly People
- **Communication skill:** Recognizing and returning friendliness
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Some people are naturally warm — they smile, say hi, include others. Learning to *spot* friendliness (and return it) helps you find good people to connect with. And here's the best part: friendliness is contagious. When you recognize warmth and warm right back, you make connections easily — and when *you're* the friendly one, you make the whole room better.
