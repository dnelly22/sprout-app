## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 5
- **Title:** Telling Stories
- **Communication skill:** Telling a story in an engaging way
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Everyone has stories — the trick is telling them so people actually *want* to listen. A good story grabs attention right away, gets to the point, includes the fun parts, and reads the room. You don't need a dramatic, exciting life; you just need to make the everyday interesting. Being a good storyteller makes you fun to be around and is one of the easiest ways to connect with people.

# MEDIUM MODE — "Reading the Room"
