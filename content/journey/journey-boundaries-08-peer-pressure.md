## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 8
- **Title:** Peer Pressure
- **Communication skill:** Recognizing and refusing peer pressure
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Peer pressure is when people push you to do something you don't really want to do — just because they're doing it, or to fit in. It can be a little (a silly dare) or a lot (something risky or wrong). The good news: you can learn to spot peer pressure and say no to it — clearly, confidently, and without losing your cool. You don't have to do something just because "everyone" is.
