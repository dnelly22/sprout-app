## Scenario header

- **Category:** Teasing · **Scenario #:** 4
- **Title:** Teased About Your Height
- **Communication skill:** Agree-and-disarm (owning a trait you can't change)
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When someone teases you about something true that you can't change — like your height — the most powerful move is to *agree with it,* confidently. "Yep, I'm short!" leaves the teaser with nowhere to go. They wanted you to feel bad about it, so when you're totally fine with it, the tease just flops. You can't change your height. But you can absolutely own it.
