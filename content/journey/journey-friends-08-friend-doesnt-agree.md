## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 8
- **Title:** Friend Doesn't Agree
- **Communication skill:** Respecting different opinions
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Friends won't always like the same things or agree with you — and that's actually a *good* thing. Respecting a different opinion means you can disagree and still be friends. You don't have to win, convince them, or get upset. The trick is knowing the difference between "we just like different stuff" (totally fine to disagree forever) and the rare times something really matters (where you hold your ground kindly).
