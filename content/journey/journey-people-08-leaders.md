- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 8
- **Title:** Leaders
- **Communication skill:** Recognizing, supporting, and being a good leader
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  A real leader isn't the bossiest or loudest person — it's the one who guides, includes everyone, listens, and lifts people up.
