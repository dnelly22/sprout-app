## Scenario header

- **Category:** Boundaries · **Scenario #:** 7
- **Title:** Sharing Secrets
- **Communication skill:** Keeping safe secrets — and telling unsafe ones
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  When a friend trusts you with a secret, keeping it makes you a trustworthy friend — so don't gossip or spread what's told to you in confidence. BUT here's the important part: not every secret should be kept. Some "secrets" are unsafe — if a secret is about someone being hurt, in danger, or someone using "keep this secret" to do something harmful, that's a secret you SHOULD tell a trusted adult. Knowing the difference is one of the most important skills there is.
