## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 5
- **Title:** Sharing
- **Communication skill:** Cooperation and kindness
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Sharing isn't just a rule grown-ups make you follow — it's one of the fastest ways to make playing fun and turn other kids into friends. But real sharing has two sides most kids only learn one of: being *generous* (offering, taking turns, not hogging) **and** being *fair to yourself* (it's okay to keep something special, or say "that's all I can give"). You can be kind and still have limits. The magic is using your words to do both.
