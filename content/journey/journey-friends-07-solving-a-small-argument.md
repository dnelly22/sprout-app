## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 7
- **Title:** Solving a Small Argument
- **Communication skill:** Conflict resolution
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Here's a surprise: even *best* friends argue. Arguing doesn't mean a friendship is broken — it's totally normal. What actually matters is *how you fix it.* Solving an argument means staying calm instead of blowing up, listening to the other side, finding a fair solution, and not letting one small thing turn into a giant thing. The kids with the strongest friendships aren't the ones who never argue — they're the ones who know how to make up.
