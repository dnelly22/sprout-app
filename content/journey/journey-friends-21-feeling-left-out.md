## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 21
- **Title:** Feeling Left Out
- **Communication skill:** Handling the sting of being left out
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Feeling left out — when others are doing something without you, or you're on the outside of a group — is one of the most common and most painful feelings there is. It can make you feel unwanted or "not enough." But feeling left out doesn't mean you're unlikable, and there are healthy ways to handle it: you can speak up, include yourself, find your people, and remember that one moment of being left out isn't the whole story about you.
