## Scenario header

- **Category:** People · **Scenario #:** 6
- **Title:** Mean People
- **Communication skill:** Reading meanness and protecting yourself
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Some people act mean — and it usually says more about what's going on inside THEM (pain, insecurity, a need to feel powerful) than about you. Understanding that helps you not take it personally. But understanding *why* someone's mean doesn't mean you have to accept being treated badly. Read meanness for what it is, protect yourself, don't become mean back, and get help when it turns into real cruelty or bullying.
