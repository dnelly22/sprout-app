## Scenario header

- **Category:** Talking · **Scenario #:** 11
- **Title:** Talking to Adults
- **Communication skill:** Speaking confidently and respectfully with grown-ups
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Talking to grown-ups you don't know well can feel intimidating — but it's a hugely useful skill: asking a teacher for help, ordering for yourself, thanking a coach, asking a librarian a question, and (most important of all) telling a trusted adult when something's wrong. The keys: make eye contact, speak clearly, be polite, and remember that adults are usually happy to help a kid who asks nicely. A confident, respectful kid impresses grown-ups.
