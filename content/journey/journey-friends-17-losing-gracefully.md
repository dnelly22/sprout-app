## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 17
- **Title:** Losing Gracefully
- **Communication skill:** Handling disappointment & sportsmanship
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Losing stinks — there's no way around it. But how you handle losing says a *lot* about you. A gracious loser congratulates the winner, handles the disappointment without melting down or blaming, and tries again. Throwing a fit, accusing people of cheating, or quitting makes losing feel even worse — and makes people not want to play with you. Everybody loses sometimes. Handling it well is a genuine superpower.
