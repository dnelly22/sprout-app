## Scenario header

- **Category:** Friends · **Scenario #:** 16
- **Title:** Winning Gracefully
- **Communication skill:** Humility & good sportsmanship
- **Why this skill matters (kid-facing):**
  Winning feels amazing — but *how* you win matters as much as whether you win. A gracious winner celebrates without rubbing it in, gives the other person credit, and stays humble. A sore winner — gloating, bragging, teasing the loser — makes everyone not want to play with them. Here's the secret: people remember a good sport way longer than they remember the score. Nobody likes a show-off, but everyone loves a good winner.
